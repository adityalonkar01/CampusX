About the platform

A scalable digital foundation for connected campus management.

CampusX is the frontend foundation of a role-based College Management System. It is designed to evolve into a secure PHP and MySQL application with independent administrative portals.

01

Independent roles

Student Admin, Faculty Admin, CR Admin, Council Admin and CRC Admin remain separate logical roles.

02

Future-ready backend

The frontend does not pretend to provide production authorization. Server-side authentication and permission checks will be added later.

03

Modular expansion

Future modules such as examinations, fees, library, attendance, placements and complaints can be added without redesigning the foundation.
